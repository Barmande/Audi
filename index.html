About Audi

Audi is one of the world's most prestigious automotive manufacturers, dedicated to the art of engineering luxury, performance, and innovation. Headquartered in Ingolstadt, Bavaria, Audi vehicles are crafted across 9 world-class facilities spanning 8 European countries — each one a testament to German precision and excellence. Since 1964, Audi has stood as a cornerstone of the Volkswagen Group, consistently setting the benchmark for what a luxury automobile can and should be.

Audi company

Nestled in the heart of Bavaria, the Ingolstadt plant serves as far more than a production facility — it is the beating heart of Audi's engineering universe and the global headquarters of the Volkswagen Group's technical development. Here, iconic models such as the Audi A3, A4, A5, and Q2 are brought to life with meticulous precision. In 2022 alone, 332,981 vehicles rolled off its assembly lines, supported by a workforce of 88,000 — making Audi the largest employer in the entire region. As the automotive world shifts toward an electric future, Ingolstadt leads the charge: a dedicated battery assembly line is already under construction for the highly anticipated Audi Q6 e-tron, with the plant's flexible production lines ensuring a seamless transition into the era of zero-emission luxury. Audi is not just a car — it's a Lifestyle. Founded on July 16, 1909, by August Horch in Zwickau, Germany, Audi has grown into one of the most iconic names in automotive history. Alongside Mercedes-Benz and BMW, it forms the legendary trinity of German luxury — three brands that have collectively redefined what it means to drive with prestige, power, and purpose.

Audi Australia Building

Why Visit Audi Centre Sydney? Even for those not currently in the market for a new car, the center is a must-visit destination. With its luxury cafes, sophisticated atmosphere, and a front-row seat to the future of automotive design, it offers a premium lifestyle experience in one of Australia’s most iconic cities.

Financial Support

Audi: More Than Just a Carmaker Audi is not merely an automobile manufacturer; it is one of the global giants of Sponsorship in the worlds of sports and culture. Audi’s strategy is to consistently align itself with "the best" and "the most luxurious," keeping its famous slogan alive: Vorsprung durch Technik (Progress through Technology). Here are some of the most important sectors where Audi provides significant financial support: 1. Football (Europe’s Giants) For years, Audi has been a strategic partner to world-class football clubs. Interestingly, Audi is not just a sponsor but also a shareholder in some clubs: FC Bayern Munich: Audi is one of the club's primary owners, holding 8.33% of the shares. Every year, Bayern Munich players are gifted the latest electric or RS models. Real Madrid & FC Barcelona: Until a few years ago, Audi was the main sponsor for these two clubs, and all their stars drove Audi vehicles to their training sessions. 2. Winter Sports (Ski World Cup) For over 20 years, Audi has been the title sponsor of the International Ski Federation (FIS). Why skiing? Because Audi wants to showcase the power of its Quattro (all-wheel drive) system on snow and ice. The "Audi FIS Ski World Cup" is one of the most-watched winter sports events in the world.

The A-Series stands as the definitive expression of Audi's engineering philosophy — a lineup that has shaped the very definition of the premium sedan segment for nearly three decades. The A4, one of the most decorated executive cars in automotive history, and the A3, a benchmark in the compact luxury class, together form the commercial and cultural foundation upon which Audi's global reputation was built. From the precision- A1 to the hand-crafted refinement of the A8 — Audi's flagship limousine — every model in this series embodies a singular principle: that true luxury is not a feature, but a standard.

Audi A1

The Audi A1 is a premium supermini launched in 2010. It is the smallest model in the Audi lineup, designed for urban driving with high-quality interior materials and efficient engines, competing with cars like the Mini Hatch.

Audi A2

Produced between 1999 and 2005, the Audi A2 was a revolutionary compact MPV. It was known for its all-aluminum construction, which made it incredibly light and fuel-efficient, though it was ahead of its time in terms of design.

Audi A3

The Audi A3 is a subcompact executive car that has been in production since 1996. It shares its platform with the Volkswagen Golf and is available in various body styles, including hatchback, sedan, and convertible.

Audi A4

As one of Audi's most popular models, the A4 is a compact executive car produced since 1994. It is widely recognized for its balance of luxury, advanced technology, and the legendary Quattro all-wheel-drive system.

Audi A5

The Audi A5 is a series of executive coupe and sportback models launched in 2007. It focuses on elegant styling and performance, providing a more sporty alternative to the traditional A4 sedan.

Audi A6

The Audi A6 is a mid-size luxury executive car that offers a spacious interior and high-end tech features. It has been a core part of Audi's lineup since 1994, competing directly with the BMW 5 Series and Mercedes-Benz E-Class.

Audi A7

The A7 is a luxury four-door sportback known for its sleek, sloping roofline. Launched in 2010, it combines the practicality of a sedan with the aggressive styling of a coupe and a high-performance interior.

Audi A8

The Audi A8 is the flagship full-size luxury sedan. Since 1994, it has been the showcase for Audi's newest innovations, featuring an aluminum chassis (Audi Space Frame) and the highest levels of luxury and comfort.

The S-Series is where Audi's engineering ambition transcends the boundaries of conventional luxury. Born from the same bloodline as Audi's legendary quattro rally heritage, each S model represents the perfect synthesis of refined elegance and raw, uncompromising performance. The S3 redefines the everyday sports car, the S4 commands the executive segment with quiet authority, and the S8 — Audi's ultimate performance flagship — delivers supercar acceleration wrapped in the discretion of a grand limousine. The S-Series does not ask you to choose between luxury and power. It renders the question irrelevant.

Audi S1

The smallest powerhouse in Audi's lineup, the S1 packs immense performance into a compact frame. Equipped with the legendary quattro all-wheel-drive system, it delivers lightning-fast acceleration and agile handling, making it a "pocket rocket" for those who love high-speed urban driving.

Audi S2

The pioneer that launched the S-series legacy and the spiritual successor to the legendary "Audi Quattro." Featuring a renowned turbocharged 5-cylinder engine and a unique exhaust note, the S2 is an icon of Audi’s enduring 90s engineering. Thanks to its massive tuning potential and iconic quattro drivetrain, it remains a cult classic among automotive enthusiasts worldwide.

Audi S3

The S3 is the ultimate "everyday sports car." It strikes an impeccable balance between a practical family vehicle and a high-performance machine. Whether you're commuting to the office or carving through mountain passes, the S3 offers an exhilarating experience without sacrificing daily usability.

Audi S4

A true icon among sports sedans, the S4 has long been the symbol of Audi’s understated power. Focused on precision engineering and superior stability, it provides a professional-grade driving experience wrapped in a sophisticated and elegant design.

Audi S5

A breathtaking combination of coupe aesthetics and S-series muscle. With its sweeping lines and aggressive stance, the S5 is designed for those who want to showcase their style and prestige on the road while enjoying immense power and torque.

Audi S6

A business-class athlete with a powerful heart. The S6 is tailored for executives who require the space and comfort of the A6 but want the soul of a supercar. It’s a discreet powerhouse that transforms from a refined cruiser to a high-speed performer at the touch of the pedal.

Audi S7

The pinnacle of design in the S range, this four-door Sportback captivates with its aggressive, aerodynamic silhouette. The S7 is the embodiment of "sporty luxury," crafted for those who refuse to compromise between stunning beauty and raw speed.

Audi S8

Audi’s flagship "mobile palace" with breathtaking performance. The S8 takes the ultra-luxury features of the A8 and pairs them with a massive engine capable of propelling this grand sedan to incredible speeds in seconds. It is the ultimate expression of "stealth wealth" and hidden power.

With the engine screaming inches behind your head and the open sky above, it delivers a sensory experience that no closed cabin can replicate. This is not transportation — this is theatre. And then there is the R8 V10. A naturally aspirated 5.2-litre V10 — no turbos, no shortcuts, no compromise. Just ten cylinders of pure mechanical fury, spinning to 8,700 RPM and producing a sound that has been described as the closest thing to a Formula 1 car that money can legally put on a public road. In an era where the industry has abandoned high-revving naturally aspirated engines in favour of efficiency, the R8 V10 stood as a defiant act of rebellion. It is Audi's greatest achievement. It is one of the last of its kind. And for those reasons alone, it will never be forgotten.

Audi R8 Coupe

The embodiment of pure engineering and Audi’s bold entry into the supercar elite. Featuring an ultra-lightweight aluminum space frame and the iconic "Sideblades," the R8 Coupe strikes a perfect balance between everyday usability and Le Mans-winning performance. It is crafted for those who demand track-proven technology wrapped in a sophisticated, timeless silhouette.

Audi R8 Spyder

An unfiltered symphony of power; the Spyder is the open-top variant that elevates driving excitement to a sensory peak. By removing the roof, the barrier between driver and machine vanishes, allowing the roar of the engine right behind your head to create a dramatic, unforgettable atmosphere. It is the choice for those who seek absolute freedom and high-profile style under the open sky.

Audi R8 V10

The legendary V10; the beating heart that secured the R8’s place in history. This naturally aspirated 10-cylinder masterpiece is one of the final icons of automotive engineering, delivering brutal power and a high-pitched scream reminiscent of classic Formula 1 cars. The V10 badge is a signature of absolute dominance and a tribute to the golden era of high-displacement engines, offering endless, linear acceleration.
